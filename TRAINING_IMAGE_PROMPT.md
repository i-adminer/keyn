# ChatGPT Image Generation Prompt for Training Section

## Prompt:

Create a professional, modern illustration for a Training & Development section on a corporate website. The image should convey:

**Main Elements:**
- A professional training/workshop setting with diverse professionals
- Clean, modern aesthetic with a corporate feel
- Subtle elements suggesting learning and development (notebooks, presentations, collaborative discussion)
- Professional color palette with royal blue (#1e3a8a) and gold/orange (#f59e0b) accents

**Style:**
- Modern flat illustration or semi-realistic style
- Clean lines and professional composition
- Bright, inviting atmosphere
- High quality, web-ready (landscape orientation, 800x600px minimum)

**Mood:**
- Professional yet approachable
- Growth-focused and collaborative
- Confident and empowering
- Corporate but not stiff

**Context:**
This image is for Keyn People Advisory's Training & Development section, representing services like supervisory skills, leadership development, HR fundamentals, performance management, and workplace professionalism training.

**Technical Requirements:**
- PNG format with transparent or white background
- Landscape orientation (4:3 or 16:9 ratio)
- Minimum 800x600px resolution
- Professional quality suitable for a corporate website
